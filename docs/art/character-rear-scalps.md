# Rear-facing character hair

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool on 2026-10-11. Runtime asset:
`unity_project/Assets/Resources/Characters/Modular/RearScalps.png`.
The generated RGBA atlas is copied unchanged. Two tight sprite rectangles are
selected in code, without editing the generated pixels. Hair colour comes from
the player's existing `hc` appearance value. Existing hairstyles, buns, ornaments,
robes and weapons remain part of the modular character.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a production game sprite atlas of TWO rear-facing HAIR SCALP overlays for a Chinese xianxia painted paper-doll character, male left and female right. Create a transparent background atlas with two equal side-by-side cells. Both sprites are viewed exactly from BEHIND: the rounded back of the skull covered entirely by brushed hair strands, no face, no eyes, no mouth, no ears, no front fringe. Only the scalp / back of head from crown to nape; NO bun, no hat, no hair pin, no shoulders, no torso, no long hanging hair. The existing game will overlay its chosen bun, hair ornaments and long hair around this cap. Male cap slightly squarer, female cap slightly narrower and rounded. Elegant hand-painted detailed soft linework matching traditional Chinese RPG illustrations, grayscale silver-gray strands with deep charcoal shadows and silvery highlights, designed for runtime multiplicative hair-color tinting. Neutral studio overhead light, subtle convincing round volume and many clearly visible flowing hair strands from crown down to nape. Exactly TWO isolated scalp sprites, centered one in each equal cell, same vertical alignment and size, occupy 70 percent of cell height, with generous transparent margins. Flat front-facing camera looking at BACK of skull, NOT three-quarter, no labels or text, no border, no checkerboard. Actual transparent alpha around the two hair caps.
